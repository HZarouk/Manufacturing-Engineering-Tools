Free Manufacturing Engineering Calculators

Use these practical tools to estimate production capacity, manpower requirements and key manufacturing performance indicators. Enter your own data to explore results directly in your browser, without creating an account.

Machine Capacity and Manpower Planning

The capacity planner estimates required machine-hours, the number of machines needed, operator positions, effective daily capacity and the capacity gap against demand. Results depend on the cycle time, available production time, OEE assumption and flexibility buffer you enter.

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)

The OEE calculator estimates Availability, Performance and Quality, then combines them to calculate Overall Equipment Effectiveness. Use consistent planned production time, downtime, ideal cycle time and production counts when comparing results.

Takt Time and Cycle Time

Takt time represents the production pace required to meet customer demand. Compare it with actual cycle time to help identify whether a process may struggle to meet the required output. The calculator also estimates theoretical and adjusted daily capacity.

Scrap Rate and Quality Performance

The scrap rate calculator compares rejected units with total production and checks the result against a target. Confirm that the denominator and treatment of rework match your site's approved KPI definition.

These calculators provide estimates for planning and learning. Validate calculations against your plant's approved KPI definitions, product mix, process constraints and production data before making operational decisions.
